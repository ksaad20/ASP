# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and community members pledge to make participation in Autonomous Synthesis Planner a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to fostering an open, inclusive, and collaborative environment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

Our shared goal is to build high-quality open scientific software through constructive collaboration and mutual respect.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

* Being respectful and considerate.
* Welcoming new contributors.
* Providing constructive and actionable feedback.
* Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Focusing discussions on technical and scientific topics.
* Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
* Helping others improve through mentorship and collaboration.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment or intimidation.
* Personal attacks or insulting language.
* Discrimination of any kind.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions or development.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic environment.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Documentation
* Community forums
* Project-related communication channels

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

# Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. When appropriate, maintainers may issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban individuals whose behavior is inconsistent with these standards.

---

# Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the contact methods listed in the repository.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality whenever possible.

---

# Enforcement Guidelines

When evaluating reported behavior, maintainers may consider factors such as:

* Severity of the behavior.
* Whether the behavior was repeated.
* Impact on community members.
* Willingness of the individual to correct the behavior.

Potential responses include:

1. Private warning.
2. Request for corrective action.
3. Temporary suspension from project participation.
4. Permanent removal from project spaces.

---

# Community Expectations

We encourage contributors to:

* Assume good intentions.
* Ask questions before making assumptions.
* Focus on solving technical problems.
* Keep discussions evidence-based.
* Respect scientific disagreement.
* Value reproducibility, transparency, and collaboration.

Healthy technical debate is encouraged. Personal conflict is not.
